# v0.0.1 Teleprompter — Minimalist iOS & iPadOS Teleprompter

**Teleprompter** (`v0.0.1`) is a minimalist iOS & iPadOS teleprompter, live camera studio, video trimmer, and native Home Screen widget suite built with a strict two-color **Warm Paper (`#F5F5F3`)** and **Soft Ink (`#222220`)** aesthetic and simple everyday language.

---

## Key Features

- **Native iOS & iPadOS Home Screen Widgets (`TeleprompterWidgets.appex`)**:
  When you sideload and install the `.ipa` on your iPhone or iPad, long-press your Home Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget**, and select **Teleprompter** to add 4 minimalist widgets to your Home Screen or Lock Screen:
  1. **Quick Script Reader** (*Small 2×2, Medium 4×2, Lock Screen*): Shows your pinned script title, word count, reading time, and a one-tap **Read** button (`teleprompter://read`).
  2. **Camera & Voice Glide** (*Small 2×2 & Medium 4×2*): Jump straight into **Front Cam**, **Back Cam**, or **Voice Glide** (`teleprompter://camera`, `teleprompter://voiceglide`).
  3. **Reading Speed Dial** (*Small 2×2, Medium 4×2, Lock Screen Circular*): Minimalist WPM ring dial that opens the reader in one tap.
  4. **Script Preview Card** (*Large 4×4*): Read your opening lines right on your Home Screen with **+ New** and **Read Now ▶** actions.
- **Voice Glide & Settings**: Smoothly scrolls your script at your natural speaking pace with adjustable **Glide Smoothing**, **Mic Sensitivity**, **Background Noise Filter**, **Pause Hold Time**, **Voice Language**, and **Auto-Resume**.
- **Precision Settings**: Fine `0.5 WPM` speed steps, smooth start/stop ramp, adjustable **Eye-Line Position** & **Band Height**, **Start Countdown**, **Lock Speed to Target Time**, and **Loop Script**.
- **Live Adjustable Script Area While Recording**: Drag the script overlay anywhere over the camera viewfinder or resize its **Width** and **Height** live while recording.
- **External Display & Livestream Connection**: Clean HDMI / USB-C / AirPlay external screen output with independent mirror flips, plus **RTMP / SRT / NDI Livestream** connection settings.
- **Remote Control & Settings**: Wireless Bluetooth keyboard, clicker, foot pedal, and second-device Wi-Fi remote controls with customizable keys.
